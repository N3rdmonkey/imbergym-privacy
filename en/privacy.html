§ 02Principle: your data stays on your device

ImberGym stores all data exclusively on your device. There is no user account and no server operated by the provider. Data is neither sold nor shared. No advertising, analytics or tracking tools are included.

The only internet connection is the optional barcode lookup at Open Food Facts (see § 05).

§ 03Data the app processes and why

You enter all of the following data yourself or create it by using the app. It is stored only on your device and used exclusively for the app’s features.

  • Profile: name, gender (including “not specified”), year of birth, weight, height, goal, activity level, training location and days, language and units – for greetings, calorie goal and calculations (e.g. bodyweight exercises).
  • Training: plans, workouts, sets, weights, reps, times, machine settings and the records and statistics calculated from them.
  • Body values: weight, body fat and body measurements.
  • Progress photos: only if you take or select them. They are kept in a private app folder, don’t appear in the gallery and are excluded from Android backups.
  • Nutrition (can be switched off): foods, diary entries, your own recipes and recipe photos, calorie and macro goals.
  • Supplements: your own products, intakes and reminder times.
  • Location (GPS) and air pressure: only during an outdoor recording you started – for distance, pace and elevation. The waypoints of the recording are stored.
  • Heart rate from Health Connect: only if you use “Load heart rate from watch” and allow it. ImberGym reads only heart rate for the time of a workout (e.g. from your watch), stores it locally with the workout and shows average, maximum and a curve. Nothing is written to Health Connect and nothing is shared. You can revoke the permission in Health Connect at any time.
  • Weight and body fat from Health Connect: only if you turn on “Import from Health Connect” on the “Weight & body fat” page. ImberGym then reads weight and body fat (e.g. from a Withings scale via Withings Health Mate) and stores them locally with their source. Nothing is written to Health Connect and nothing is shared; turn it off there or in Health Connect at any time.
  • Active calories from Health Connect: only if you turn on the “dynamic calorie goal”. ImberGym reads the active calories measured by your watch or phone for the last days, stores the daily total locally and calculates your daily goal from it. Nothing is written or shared.
  • Heart rate from a chest strap (Bluetooth): only if you pair a strap. During a workout the app connects directly to the strap and stores the heart rate locally with the workout (for display, heart rate zones and heart-rate-based rest). The name and identifier of the paired strap are stored as well.
  • Gyms (workout location): name and – only if you use “Use current location” – the coordinate of your gym. If you saved gyms with a location and already granted location access, the app determines your current position once when a workout starts and compares it with your gyms on the device only; the position itself is neither stored nor transmitted.
  • Premium: which product was purchased and when Google Play last confirmed the purchase – stored locally to unlock Premium.

Health data: body values, training and nutrition data may be health data within the meaning of Art. 9 GDPR. They are processed only on your device, for yourself, and are not transmitted to the provider or third parties. Processing takes place because you actively use these features (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR); you can end it at any time by deleting the data or uninstalling the app.

§ 05Data sharing and third-party services

  • Open Food Facts: when you scan a barcode that is not yet stored on your device, the app sends only the barcode number to the public Open Food Facts database (Open Food Facts, France, Open Food Facts privacy). For technical reasons your IP address is transmitted. No other data is sent. Product data is licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL).
  • Place name of a tour: after a GPS recording the app uses the Android system geocoder (Google services) to look up the place name of the starting point, e.g. “Pram, Upper Austria”. Only the coordinate of the starting point is sent to Google (Google privacy policy). The name is stored locally and can be changed or removed at any time.
  • Device calendar: only with your consent. If your calendar syncs with an account (e.g. Google Calendar), that provider’s terms apply.
  • Sharing and saving: images (e.g. Trackshow, reviews, progress photos and before/after comparisons) GPX files as well as backup files (ZIP) and CSV exports leave your device only when you send them yourself via the Android share menu to an app or storage location of your choice. A backup contains your app data and – only if you switch it on – your progress photos; keep it somewhere safe.
  • Purchases: Premium purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play. The provider receives no payment data; Google’s terms apply.
  • Android backup: if device backup is turned on, Android may store the app data (without progress photos) in your Google backup. You control this in the Android settings.

Beyond that, no data is transferred to third parties.

§ 06Data security

Data is protected by your device’s security features (screen lock, device encryption). The app stores its data in its private area, which other apps cannot access.

§ 07Your rights

  • Access: you can see all data directly in the app.
  • Rectification and erasure: you can edit or delete entries in the app; uninstalling the app deletes all data on the device.
  • Withdrawal: you can turn off permissions and calendar entries at any time.
  • Complaint: you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in Austria the Datenschutzbehörde (dsb.gv.at).

As the provider receives no data from you, it cannot hand it out or delete it for you – it is only with you.

§ 08Children

ImberGym is not directed at children under 16.

§ 09No medical advice

Calculations such as calorie goal, body fat estimate or recovery are estimates and do not replace medical advice. The app gives no dosage recommendations for supplements.

§ 10Changes to this policy

If app features change, this policy will be updated. The current version is always on this page; significant changes are announced in the Google Play Store.
