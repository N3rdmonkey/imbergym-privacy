What ImberGym does

1

Plans & templates

Your own plans with units, sets, reps and rest – or ready-made templates from beginner full body to push/pull/legs, calisthenics and yoga.

2

Active workout

One exercise per page, last values prefilled, progression hints, remembered machine settings. Rest timer even on the lock screen – can be turned off.

3

Animated exercises

Each of the 206 exercises shows its movement as a figure with the muscles involved, tempo and technique tips.

4

Records

Max weight, estimated 1RM, volume, reps, distance and pace – “New record!” right after the set.

5

Statistics & body map

Week, month or 3 months with change, distribution by muscle group, body map with recovery and weekly & yearly reviews to share.

6

Body

Weight and body fat with 7-day average, estimate from measurements and progress photos with before/after comparison – photos never leave your phone.

7

Nutrition

Calorie goal, diary with barcode scanner (Open Food Facts) and your own recipes. Can be switched off completely if you only want to train.

8

Supplements

Your own products with usual amount, “Taken” button, calendar and reminders – without dosage suggestions.

9

Calendar

If you like, every finished workout is added to your device calendar – can be turned off anytime.

Out and about

GPS recording

Running, cycling, hiking: distance, pace or speed and elevation via barometer. Keeps running with the screen off, with auto-pause at traffic lights.

Trackshow

Your tour as a camera flight over contour lines with highlights like steepest section, highest point and fastest kilometer – as an image to share.

GPX export

Every recording can be exported as a GPX file and opened in other apps.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. ImberGym works without sign-up and without its own server.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. There is no cloud, no advertising and no analytics. Details in the privacy policy.

Does the app work offline?

Yes, completely. Only looking up an unknown barcode queries Open Food Facts online.

Are my progress photos uploaded?

No. They are kept in a private app folder, don’t appear in the gallery and are even excluded from Android backups.

Can I hide nutrition?

Yes. The whole section can be switched off in the profile – ImberGym is then a pure workout log.

How do I delete my data?

Single entries directly in the app, everything at once by uninstalling the app.
